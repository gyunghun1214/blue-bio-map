## Admission rule used here

For a species-level, small-molecule MBPI entry, require a traceable chain: **source animal or alga in an original isolation/identification report → a single chemically defined compound (including the relevant stereochemistry) → matching stable CID/InChIKey or equivalent exact structure → original assay with target, biological system, endpoint, relation, raw value and unit → original article/assay ID**. A percentile also requires a fixed, sufficiently described cohort of comparable target, endpoint, assay type and test conditions. Peptides, polysaccharide fractions, extracts and crude mixtures remain separate evidence classes. An assay organism is not the compound's source organism. A missing link is recorded as missing, never filled by compound-name matching or review-derived numbers.

## Searches and attempted joins

1. **Unmerged leads:** inspected local `remotes/pr/5` research/bioactivity notes and `remotes/pr/10` bioactivity display data only as search leads; neither branch was treated as approved evidence. The PR #5 holotoxin lead prompted independent original-article and PubChem checks. The PR #5 *Saccharina japonica* Lj5 lead, DOI 10.1002/cbdv.202000233, is outside the eight candidates and is a **sulfated polysaccharide fraction** (α-glucosidase IC50 153.27 ± 22.89 µg/mL), not a single compound.
2. **Original literature:** searched each approved scientific name together with its known synonym where needed, plus `isolation`, `purified`, `compound`, `IC50`, `MIC`, target and DOI. Opened original publisher articles, PubMed primary abstracts and Europe PMC full-text XML where available. Search coverage is an audit of the records above, not proof that no other papers exist. Publication year was kept distinct from specimen/assay year; sample years were not inferred from publication dates.
3. **ChEMBL:** queried the official [ChEMBL status endpoint](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/api/data/status.json): **ChEMBL_37**, release **2026-05-01**. Queried `/api/data/document.json?doi=...` for `10.1016/j.foodchem.2018.07.142`, `10.1111/bph.16333`, `10.3390/md21010051`, `10.3390/md20050284`, `10.1021/bi00453a021`, and `10.1016/j.cbpc.2005.10.005`: **zero document rows in this version**, not evidence of biological inactivity. Queried [CHEMBL1575074 molecule](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/api/data/molecule/CHEMBL1575074.json) and [activity rows](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/api/data/activity.json?molecule_chembl_id=CHEMBL1575074&limit=1000). One row, activity **3697703**, assay **CHEMBL1613829**, document **CHEMBL1201862**, lists RECQ1 Potency **354.8 nM**, relation `=`, pChEMBL **6.45**; its document is a PubChem BioAssay dataset without wakame source provenance. A distinct [document CHEMBL1772959](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chembl/api/data/document/CHEMBL1772959.json), DOI **10.1021/np1008009**, concerns compounds from ***Sporochnus comosus***. Neither licenses transferring its activity to *Undaria* origin.
4. **PubChem:** queried official [PUG REST](https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/pug-rest) name-to-CID, CID-to-formula/InChIKey and CID-to-AID endpoints. A returned CID or AID proves a registered substance/assay link, not isolation from the candidate species. Holotoxin name collisions and fucoxanthin stereochemical/formula distinctions above prevented unsafe joins. `SAGSLVP` CID name lookup returned 404. No BioAssay activity was accepted solely because its test organism or compound name looked relevant.
5. **CMNPD:** checked [official downloads](https://docs.cmnpd.org/downloads) and inspected the 1.0 activity and 2D-structure download formats in memory. The standardized activity TSV has `COMPOUND_ID`, `TARGET_ID`, `TARGET_TYPE`, `TARGET_NAME`, `TARGET_ORGANISM`, `ACTIVITY_TYPE`, `RELATION`, `VALUE`, `UNIT`, `ASSAY_TYPE`, `ASSAY_DESCRIPTION`; it does **not** supply origin species or article DOI. The 2D SDF supplies structure and `COMPOUND_ID`, not the missing origin/publication link. Those downloaded files therefore cannot independently complete a candidate-species → compound → assay → paper chain. No bulk data was redistributed.

The [official ChEMBL pChEMBL rules](https://chembl.gitbook.io/chembl-interface-documentation/frequently-asked-questions/chembl-data-questions) require a supported concentration endpoint (`IC50`, `XC50`, `EC50`, `AC50`, `Ki`, `Kd`, `Potency`, or `ED50`), positive value in **nM**, relation **`=`**, and accepted validity status. **MIC, MFC, GI50, a single-dose viability percentage, an extract IC50, and a contraction threshold are not interchangeable with pChEMBL.** Where a valid ChEMBL pChEMBL value exists for a shared compound, it still does not establish this candidate species as its origin.
