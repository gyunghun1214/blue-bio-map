## Primary record verified for calculation

Hamel & Mercier 2013, IUCN Red List [record T180424A1629389](https://www.iucnredlist.org/species/180424/1629389), [DOI 10.2305/IUCN.UK.2013-1.RLTS.T180424A1629389.en](https://doi.org/10.2305/IUCN.UK.2013-1.RLTS.T180424A1629389.en). The **IUCN-authored assessment** text, available in a [public mirror](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/295098284_Apostichopus_japonicus_The_IUCN_Red_List_of_Threatened_Species_2013_eT180424A1629389), gives taxon *A. japonicus* (Selenka, 1867), EN A2bd v3.1, assessed May 19 **2010**, published **2013**, Northwest Pacific global native range including China, Japan, Korean Peninsula and Russian Far East. The justification cites declines and exploitation, but neither capture catches nor OBIS occurrence counts are re-used as a trend adjustment. WoRMS search-indexed [241776](https://www.marinespecies.org/aphia.php?p=taxdetails&id=241776) names *A. japonicus*. The IUCN and WoRMS primary pages responded 403 during direct retrieval; cached IUCN-authored original text and WoRMS index were used, and **newer IUCN reassessment was not established**. MCUI 80 is a project mapping of the historical EN, not a 2026 risk estimate, extinction probability, or Korean sea-area priority. Retrieval 2026-09-24 UTC. IUCN [terms](https://www.iucnredlist.org/terms/terms-of-use) and WoRMS text [CC BY information](https://www.marinespecies.org/about.php) apply; no PDF or proprietary underlying dataset redistributed.
